# Arte de la fábrica — niveles 4 y 5

Generado con la herramienta integrada ImageGen. Conversión posterior a WebP sin pérdidas y escalado nearest-neighbor con Sharp. Los originales anteriores se conservan.

- `taller-madera-v3.webp`: 768 × 768, 519524 bytes. Fondo sin interfaz ni personajes; se recorta con `cover`.
- `sprites-taller-v3.webp`: 1024 × 768, 469218 bytes, transparencia. Atlas de 4 columnas × 3 filas; celdas iguales de 256 × 256. Se muestra con `background-size: 400% 300%` y proporción cuadrada estable.
- Fila 1: cofre, trituradora, horno, engranaje.
- Fila 2: supervisor, aprendiz, clasificador, molino automático.
- Fila 3: diamante, dinamita, carbón, fuego.

La interfaz, los contadores y las instrucciones son HTML, no texto incrustado en las imágenes. Las animaciones se activan con los estados existentes del componente y respetan `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Prompt del fondo

Create a production-ready background asset for DragonCode, a retro 16-bit pixel-art browser educational game. Output ONE square 1024x1024 image. Scene: interior of an old wooden mining factory inside a purple-brown rock cavern, inspired by SNES adventure games. Heavy dark chestnut timber uprights at far left and right, horizontal wooden beams, simple large wooden cogwheels near the upper corners, a couple of small stone hearths with amber fires at the far edges about halfway down, a rear wooden gallery in the upper third, muted plum cave wall behind. Floor of rough wooden planks across bottom quarter. STRICT COMPOSITION: center 70 percent is spacious quiet dark wall, no foreground objects, no character, no conveyor, no machine, no items, no labels, no lettering, no UI. Gameplay sprites will be overlaid over middle/lower section so do not fill it. HUD overlays top 12 percent and mission panel bottom 18 percent. Balanced front-on orthographic side-view stage, no exaggerated perspective. Art should look genuinely low-resolution: chunky deliberate pixel clusters, limited palette of about 24 muted browns #56392e #302329, purple-black #18131a, amber #d89145, occasional desaturated teal. Dark readable silhouettes, hand-tiled texture restrained. NO glossy metal steampunk, NO cyan glowing sci-fi machinery, NO hyperdetail, NO realistic gradients, NO blur, NO painted brush strokes. All edges stepped pixels. Background must remain intelligible when displayed at 350px wide on mobile. Central composition crop-safe on narrow screens. No border or watermark.

## Prompt del atlas

Produce ONE transparent PNG game sprite atlas for a retro SNES 16-bit wooden mining factory browser game, canvas 1024x768, EXACT regular 4 columns by 3 rows, each cell 256x256. Each sprite contained in its cell, centered horizontally, baseline at cell y=224, generous transparent 24px margins. NO grid lines, NO labels or text, NO background, NO shadows outside sprite, genuine alpha transparency. Chunky low resolution pixel art as if each cell originally 64x64 then nearest-neighbor upscaled 4x. Muted chestnut timber and charcoal iron, russet, warm ochre, teal only for gems, black pixel outlines. NOT glossy highly detailed AI steampunk, no photorealism, no gradients, no halos. MATCH consistent palette, pixel size, side/front three-quarter view, clear small-screen silhouettes. EXACT row-major contents: row 1 col1 an open small wooden chest with a few cyan diamonds; row1 col2 a simple squat wood-framed stone crusher with two visible dark cog rollers; row1 col3 a squat brick stone furnace with a bright orange opening and short chimney; row1 col4 a simple large wooden cogwheel. Row2 col1 friendly short green goblin male mining foreman with leather apron, ochre cap and clipboard, full body; row2 col2 friendly short green goblin female mining worker with orange bandana, leather apron and wrench, full body; row2 col3 small wood-framed sorting machine with a simple iron lever, wooden gear and tray, no conveyor; row2 col4 small wood-framed automatic mill with two interlocking wooden gears and belt drive, no conveyor. Row3 col1 ONE cyan diamond crystal; row3 col2 ONE bundle of red dynamite sticks with a small fuse; row3 col3 ONE chunk of charcoal with gray edge highlights; row3 col4 a small orange campfire flame. All objects occupy their cells without touching, sprite boundaries must not cross cell boundaries. Used as CSS background sprites in a responsive game scene.
